# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SonicFetch to be an open, respectful, and collaborative project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Disruptive trolling or bad-faith interactions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may moderate discussions and contributions to keep the community safe and productive.

## Reporting conduct issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers.
